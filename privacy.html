Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

NC Publisher ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the Namaste Chart and Kal Subah Tak channels to manage their own YouTube channels and Instagram accounts. It has no other users.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube (via YouTube API Services): the owner's channel details, the owner's videos and their metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, privacy status, schedule), the owner's channel analytics, and comments on the owner's videos. The app uploads videos and thumbnails that the owner has created.
  • Instagram / Facebook (via Meta Graph API): the owner's Instagram professional accounts and linked Facebook Pages, to publish the owner's own posts and Reels.

How the data is used

Only to publish, schedule and manage the owner's own content, and to show the owner their own channel performance. Data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Where the data is stored

Access tokens and the small amount of data the app keeps (post schedule, post links, status logs) are stored only on the owner's personal computer. Nothing is stored on any external server operated by the app.

Third parties

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Instagram/Facebook data is covered by the Meta Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page and from Facebook Settings → Business integrations. Revoking access stops the app immediately; the locally stored tokens are deleted by the owner from their computer. Any locally stored data is deleted within 30 days of revocation.
