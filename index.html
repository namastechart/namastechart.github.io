NC Publisher

NC Publisher is a private, single-owner tool used by the creator of the Namaste Chart and Kal Subah Tak channels to publish and manage their own videos and posts on YouTube and Instagram.

What it does

  • Uploads and schedules the owner's own videos, Shorts and Reels.
  • Updates titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails of the owner's own videos.
  • Reads the owner's own channel statistics and comments.

It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's accounts.
